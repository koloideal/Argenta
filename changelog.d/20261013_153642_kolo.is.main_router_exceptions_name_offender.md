### Fixed

- `RepeatedTriggerNameException` and `RepeatedFlagNameException` now
  name what collided: the duplicated trigger(s), or the duplicated
  flag(s) together with the command trigger. The offending names are
  also available as `repeated_triggers` / `repeated_flags` and
  `trigger` attributes.
