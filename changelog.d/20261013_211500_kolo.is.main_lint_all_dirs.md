### Changed

- ``ruff``, ``flake8 --format=wemake``, ``ruff format`` and ``mypy`` now cover ``tests/``, ``metrics/`` and ``examples/`` in addition to ``src/`` — in the ``justfile`` recipes and in the ``ruff``/``mypy``/``wps`` CI workflows. mypy keeps ``--strict`` for ``src`` and uses its default mode for the rest.
- ``wemake-python-styleguide`` codes are turned off via ``per-file-ignores`` for ``tests/``, ``metrics/`` and ``examples/`` — its style rules conflict with pytest/benchmark idioms; real checks (F, E, W codes) still run there.

### Fixed

- Violations reported on the newly covered directories: unused lambdas assigned to variables, unused variable assignments, star imports in ``metrics/benchmarks/__init__.py`` (replaced by explicit re-exports with ``__all__``), overlong lines, ``pytest.CaptureFixture`` imports, ambiguous ``pytest.raises`` match patterns, missing ``strict=`` in ``zip()``, append-loops replaced with comprehensions, and two ``type: ignore[arg-type]`` on intentional negative tests. Also removed stale ``pyright``/``pyrefly`` ignore comments left in ``tests/``.
