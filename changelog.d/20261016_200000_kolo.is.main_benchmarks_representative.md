### Changed

- Benchmarks now measure the operation in isolation: fixture setup
  (router/app construction, input parsing) moved to module level instead
  of being timed together with the measured call. Reported times reflect
  the actual hot paths rather than registration overhead.
- `pre_cycle_setup` benchmarks are renamed to `app_startup` — they measure
  the full app startup unit (`App()` + router registration + pre-cycle
  setup), which is what users actually pay at launch.

### Added

- New benchmark types for previously unmeasured hot paths:
  `command_dispatch` (end-to-end cost of one typed command, including the
  unknown-command suggestion path), `command_groups_render` (description
  block rendering — paid on every command when
  `repeat_command_groups_printing` is enabled) and `command_autocomplete`
  (per-keystroke completion over command and history sets).
