### Changed

- A wrong type hint on a handler's first argument now emits a standard
  `UserWarning` (via `warnings.warn`) pointing at the handler
  registration line, instead of printing a Rich-formatted message to
  stdout. It can now be filtered, turned into an error, or asserted in
  tests with `pytest.warns`.
